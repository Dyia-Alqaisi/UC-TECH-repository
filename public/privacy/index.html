Enquiry privacy

When you submit a consultation request, United Cloud Technology receives your name, company, email, phone number, selected service and message.

How we use your details

We use these details to respond to your enquiry and manage follow-up. Requests are stored in the website database and may be included in email notifications to our team. This form does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Access and retention

Access is restricted to authorised administrators and the providers operating the website and email service. We keep enquiry details while needed to respond and manage the business relationship. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of your enquiry.

Spam prevention

We store a keyed hash derived from the submitting network address to limit repeated submissions. It is not displayed in the request dashboard. Administrative sign-in attempts are limited for account protection.
