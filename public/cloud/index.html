Can the cloud environment serve users across the Middle East?+

Yes. The architecture can be planned for access across Middle East markets. Final performance and hosting locations depend on the selected provider, region and connectivity design.

How much do cloud servers cost?+

Pricing depends on the provider, server configuration, region, storage, data transfer and usage. A consultation is used to define the requirements before quotation.

Are backup and recovery included?+

Backup and recovery are available cloud capabilities. The retention period, recovery targets, coverage and management responsibilities are defined for each engagement.
