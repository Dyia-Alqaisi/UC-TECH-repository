Good to know

Your questions,
answered.

Can an assistant use our company information?+

Approved business content can inform the solution. Data access, permissions, integration and the scope of answers need to be agreed for the project.

Can AI be guaranteed to be correct every time?+

No. Outputs need evaluation, suitable safeguards and human review where the consequences of an error matter.

Do we need a complete dataset before we talk?+

You can start by describing the task and available information. Data suitability and any gaps can then be discussed.
